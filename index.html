DECENTRALIZED ARCHITECTURE

How AirLink Pulls It Off
The Physics Of Jammer-Proof P2P

No magic tricks. Just robust wireless engineering using the built-in radios already inside your phone.

01

Zero-Touch Peer Discovery

AirLink continuously broadcasts low-energy discovery beacons over Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi Direct. Phones detect nearby peers within seconds without requiring manual pairing or internet connectivity.

val state = meshRouter.discoverNearbyPeers()
02

Self-Healing MeshRouter

If you want to talk to someone 200 meters away, your packet hops through intermediate devices standing between you. Each device acts as an autonomous repeater node with intelligent TTL (Time-To-Live) and loop prevention.

MeshPacket(src, dest, hopCount = n+1, ttl = 7)
03

Real-Time Voice Streaming

Full-duplex audio captured through ActiveCallService and streamed in low-latency 20ms chunks. Talk directly to your friends with walkie-talkie speed even in the deepest subway or remote forest.

ActiveCallService.streamPcmAudio(socket)
04

Cryptographic Privacy

All packets are signed and encrypted. Intermediate relay nodes forward packets blindly without ever having the keys to decrypt your private voice or read your private messages.

CryptoManager.encryptPayload(peerPublicKey)
05

Duplicate Flood Suppression

To prevent battery drain and radio clutter, seenPacketCache drops duplicate packets instantly. Your battery consumption remains under 2% per hour even in massive crowd gatherings.

if (seenPacketCache.contains(id)) return drop()
06

Zero Centralized Servers

There is no master database. There is no AWS or Google Cloud server that can be seized, subpoenaed, or hacked. The network exists only between the phones physically present.

serverAddress = null // Truly serverless
